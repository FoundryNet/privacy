FoundryNet Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 24, 2026

Overview

FoundryNet Labs ("we", "us") operates data intelligence APIs and MCP servers. This policy covers how we handle data when you interact with our services, including through third-party platforms like the OpenAI GPT Store, Poe, MCPize, or direct API access.

What We Collect

When you use our APIs or GPT integrations, we receive the queries you send to our endpoints. We log query metadata (timestamp, endpoint called, response status) for operational monitoring and service improvement. We do not collect personal information, email addresses, or account details through our API endpoints.

What We Don't Collect

We do not collect, store, or process personally identifiable information (PII) beyond what is contained in the queries you voluntarily submit. We do not use cookies or tracking pixels on our API endpoints. We do not sell or share query data with third parties.

Payment Data

Payments via x402 (USDC) are processed on-chain and are publicly visible on the respective blockchain. Payments via Stripe are handled by Stripe, Inc. under their privacy policy. We do not store credit card numbers or bank account details.

MINT Attestation

Query results may be attested through the MINT Protocol, which creates a cryptographic hash of the response anchored on the Solana blockchain. Attestations contain hashes of data, not the data itself. No personal information is recorded on-chain.

Data Retention

Query logs are retained for up to 90 days for operational purposes and then deleted. Aggregated, anonymized usage statistics may be retained indefinitely.

Third-Party Platforms

When you access our services through platforms like OpenAI (GPT Store), Poe, MCPize, or others, those platforms have their own privacy policies governing your use of their services. We only receive the query data that those platforms forward to our API endpoints.
